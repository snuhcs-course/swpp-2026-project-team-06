# 손 위 감귤 크기 표현

현재 본문은 [윤곽선 손 버전](outline-prompts.md)과 비교용 손 길이18cm 텍스트를 사용한다. 자 아이콘은 제거했으며 [생성 기록](ruler-prompt.md)과 원본만 보존한다. 아래 hand-*.png는 이전 채색 버전 보존본이다.

내장 ImageGen으로 생성한 투명 PNG 일러스트 3종. 사용자 명시 요청에 따라 손/감귤의 상징 일러스트 생성 범위를 허용했다. 실제 상품 사진·규격 증빙이 아니다. 중간 크기를 원본으로 소/대 크기를 별도 편집 생성했고 원본 파일을 변형 없이 저장했다. 치수·무게는 추정하지 않는다.

## hand-small.png
최종 프롬프트:
Edit this illustration as one variant in a matched set. Shrink only the tangerine and its leaf to 65% of their current diameter, centered at the same palm point, revealing the palm beneath. It should clearly read as a SMALL tangerine relative to this same hand. Preserve the EXACT hand size, outline, finger positions, wrist angle, canvas dimensions, placement, colors, lighting and fine gouache/paper texture. Do not scale the hand or whole composition. Keep a single orange and five-finger hand. No text, measuring marks, other objects, shadows outside the illustration, or background. Transparent PNG. Symbolic size illustration, not a measured product photo.

## hand-medium.png
최종 프롬프트:
Create a standalone editorial gouache / cut-paper illustration for a Korean farm product page: one anatomically clear adult hand held palm-up, fingers gently open and relaxed, viewed obliquely from above, wrist entering from bottom-right. ONE whole tangerine sits in the center of the open palm, diameter approximately 65% of the visible palm width (medium relative size). Fruit round slightly flattened with small green leaf. Warm peach/cream hand, muted orange fruit, sage leaf. Fine tactile matte handmade-paper grain consistent with quiet food editorial icons, simple soft filled shapes, subtle painted volume, no outlines, no photorealism, no plastic 3D, no glossy glare. Plain transparent background, centered object, square canvas, whole hand and fingertips visible with generous even margins; no scenery, additional fruit, text, numerals, dimension arrows, ruler, coins or quality badges. This is a symbolic size-description illustration, not a real measurement or product photograph. Keep fruit distinct from hand, realistic five-finger anatomy.
