### Photos inside documents, redesigned edit view, input-first facts (DEV-452, local candidate)
- **Photos from documents.** In the combined setup flow, product photos inside uploaded PDFs and document images become product photos. PDF pages use embedded image positions. Other pages and screenshots use a layout heuristic that skips text-heavy areas and flat graphics. A photo repeated on several pages is kept once. Each photo records its source file, page, position and confirmation state. No external AI call is involved.
- Found photos go on the generated page right away, marked as taken from the material, with at most two per section. A sale request is refused until the producer confirms or removes every photo the page uses. Confirming does not change the page, and removing a photo keeps the analysis. Found photos do not count toward the upload allowance.
- **Pages.** The 20-page limit per PDF is removed. The 60-page limit per batch stays. Pages are processed one at a time within the existing time and memory limits.
- **Edit view order:** ① photos ② basic facts ③ sale settings summary (read-only) ④ page sections, each with its own review notes ⑤ material found but not used, with "add to page". The header shows the number of items left to check.
- **Input first.** When the producer's input and the material disagree on the same fact, the page uses the input. The material's value is shown under that fact, where the producer can switch to it or add it to the page. Composition text whose weight differs from the sale options starts off the page and does not block anything.
- **Waiting bar.** While a page is generated, a bar and percentage show progress from real work counts: pages read, text chunks analysed and finishing steps. It never estimates from elapsed time, never goes backwards and reaches 100% only when every step is done. One indivisible step, such as a single analysis call for a text file, holds its value until it finishes.
- **Text-only material.** An empty product name takes the product hint. When the sale settings have prices, an option line keeps its size and fruit count without the material's price; a price inside a sentence is never cut out, and the whole sentence stays off the page instead. Brix numbers in the material are not filled in automatically; they appear under the brix row for reference. Without photos, the page asks for a product photo and offers a button to add one.
- **After generation** (PM decision, November 27): the producer lands in the edit view. The read-only page opens from "수정한 페이지 보기".
- **Draft list.** Cards made through the combined setup show "making · N%" with a bar, "ready · N to check" or "failed". The card and "상품 수정" open the page's edit view directly. Unnamed drafts show the product hint as their name. Names read from the material drop form labels such as "1. 품목 또는 명칭". In the combined flow, a name filled in from the material is saved immediately, so just opening the page does not count as an unsaved change.
- When none of the material's box weights match the sale options (e.g. material "선물 3kg", sale 5kg), that composition text starts off the page. The server makes this call. The material's text appears in the composition row, where the producer can add it to the page.
- **Review screen redesign** (PM approval, November 27): after generation, the screen is the page as buyers will see it, and all fixing happens in sheets.
  - **Top bar:** "N to check · check one by one". The count includes photos taken from the material, sentences that need checking before publication, and unfinished reading.
  - **Edit entry points:** a photo button on the hero; "정보 고치기" by the name, which covers basic facts, material values for reference and the sale settings summary; and two controls at each section heading. "확인 N" checks that section only, and "고치기" opens an edit sheet that leaves the other sections untouched.
  - **Unused material:** a "material not used" row at the end.
  - **Check sheet:** one item at a time. Each answer is saved immediately: OK, use suggestion, edit, or remove from the page. Risky sentences (private data, health claims, other products) have no "OK".
  - **Sale request:** while items remain, the request button opens the check sheet.
  - **Preview:** the header's preview shows the plain buyer view.
- **PDF pages the reader cannot finish.** When remote reading cannot finish a page (for example, a very dense page), the text embedded in the PDF is used instead and recorded in diagnostics. Scanned or photo pages without embedded text, as well as uncertain-send, budget and auth errors, still count as unfinished and can be re-read from the top of the review screen.
- **Adding and blocking.** "＋ Add section" at the end of the review page lists sections the page does not have yet. Picking one opens it ready to type; this replaces the old "add content" chips. The top of the review page shows everything that blocks a sale request: sentences and photos to check, unfinished reading, missing sale settings, and a missing product photo, which the server requires.
- **Check sheet answers** (PM, November 27): OK / edit / remove, plus "apply suggestion" when a minimal fix such as spacing exists. Two cases trigger an offer: fixing a phrase (a change of 2–30 characters), or approving a date or time expression. The sheet then lists other open items containing the same phrase, with previews, and saves them only on "change all" / "all OK". Whole-sentence rewrites and risky items (private data, health claims, other products) are never offered, and nothing changes automatically.
- **Sale settings win** (PM, November 27):
  - **Option lines (rule b, changed by the PM on November 28):** a composition line naming a weight that is not sold leaves the page and is shown for reference under composition and price. A line about a sold weight stays even when its grade name (가정용, 대과, 실속형, …) differs from the option name, because it tells the buyer how many fruits that box holds. A weight under half the smallest option (an item inside a pack, a single fruit) is not read as an option.
  - **Harvest season (rule a):** a harvest or season sentence whose months miss the delivery window leaves the page and appears under the delivery row.
  - Both can be added back. Batches without sale settings are unchanged.
