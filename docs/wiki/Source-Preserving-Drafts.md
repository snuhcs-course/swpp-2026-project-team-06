# Source-preserving drafts (DEV-452)

The selected direction preserves producer wording, checks bounded OCR/sentence/repetition issues, and separates owner confirmation for conditional information. It does not approve every claim or release.

The new opt-in batch policy uses all current validated extraction evidence rather than citations selected by an earlier generated page. Every input is accounted for, with source revision/sanitization/quote identity. Exact source duplicates may merge; ambiguous placement, editorial concerns, conditional prices/dates/availability/dispatch and safety restrictions remain explicit. The policy does not run paid prose refinement. Existing batches retain their policy until explicitly switched. OCR correctness and unextracted source content remain outside this guarantee.

Only minimal whitespace repairs are proposed automatically. Broad grammatical correctness and semantic repetition are not established by these mechanical checks. Owners confirm or correct current information; relative dates are never guessed and prices belong in sale settings. Safety checks apply to corrected values and cannot be overridden by confirmation.

Decisions persist against individual source/scope/policy bindings and use current plan/review versions on write. Unrelated source additions preserve valid decisions; relevant changes invalidate them. Producer edits and exclusions remain protected. Private save/reopen and explicit publication use the existing editor boundary; review actions alone do not publish. Compare identical development inputs, keep held/failed items and separate automated drafts from owner-completed results. Figma follow-up DEV-430; human code/producer review and deployment remain separate.
