## What a good page should do

Keep the existing nine sections: a short product introduction; package contents/counts; taste/size; production and quality evidence; packing/delivery method; storage; appearance-related FAQ; farm history; dated farm updates. Each of the 18 modules has a specific role, including the five photo modules. Photo descriptions guide placement and do not supply public factual claims. Do not fill missing information with generic marketing copy.

Give each eligible claim a primary module and preapproved alternatives. Preserve product, option, quantity/unit, negation, approximation, measurement/sample and temporal conditions. Useful identity echoes are allowed; repeated explanations across sections are defects. Separate different products, samples and years rather than merging them to reduce repetition. Current structured origin, price and checkout fields retain their existing contracts.

Classify claims as required, optional, excluded or unresolved, with a reason. Criticality depends on the consequence of omission/error for understanding the product, quantity, quality or storage; source presence alone does not make a fact critical. Distinguish producer statements from independent verification. Unresolved labels block audited-gold acceptance and must not be silently excluded from denominators. Current exclusions include monetary amounts, sales/delivery schedules, platform policies, private data, health claims and other-product facts. Safe-clause extraction requires separate evidence validation; do not bypass rejected-candidate checks.

## Separate the measurements

Report page-wide claim preservation, correctness/qualifiers, placement, editorial quality and processing outcomes separately. A claim appearing in a different section is different from a missing claim. Duplicate text earns no additional coverage. Correct text elsewhere cannot cancel unsupported text. Keep the historical strict scorer and failed trials; new label/scorer versions must rescore both arms and show denominator changes.

Historical targets remain **at least 95% fact recall per format and zero unsupported critical/forbidden content**, with existing critical FAIL rules. These documents do not lower the targets or establish release readiness.

The proposed 1–5 rubric covers organization, repetition, Korean fluency and buyer usefulness. Proposed acceptance requires median ≥4 in each dimension, no page below 3 and zero remaining major defects, plus separate factual/processing gates. Unresolved severity blocks acceptance. These thresholds, sampling and disagreement rules **need approval before evaluation**. The proposed paired development review uses scheduled trial 1 for all 24 existing public sources, retains failures without substituting later successes, reports format counts and keeps 23 synthetic controls separate. Previously inspected experiments remain exploratory even if subsequently anonymized. With only Jinwoo assigned, report single-reviewer evidence, not independent two-person agreement.
