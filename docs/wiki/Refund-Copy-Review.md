# Refund wording review

DEV-469 replaces prominent Korean “full refund” labels with calmer, action-oriented language across consumer and producer screens. The deadline field becomes “Final shipment date”; declining a changed delivery period uses “Cancel reservation” followed by “Cancel and get your payment back.”

Explanations continue to state that the entire paid amount is returned, without cancellation fees, by the farm to the original account within the existing three-business-day period. Amounts, refund eligibility, deadlines, actors, order states and API behavior do not change. Formal legal documents, consent versions and previously stored messages are unchanged.

The same display labels are used in product draft summaries, new refund reasons, Mock responses and validation feedback. Producer date fields include a subdued explanation of what happens if shipment does not occur by that date.
