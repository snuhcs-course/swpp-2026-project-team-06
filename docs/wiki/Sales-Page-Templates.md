# Sales page template prototypes

DEV-52 is an isolated design prototype for FEAT-03/04/07. It introduces three editable layouts: Farm Store, Farm Photo Story, and Farmer Story. The same user-supplied text and local photos are retained when switching layouts.

No product photos, people, farms, quality claims, or sales copy are generated. Missing optional content is omitted. Image generation is limited to a prebuilt abstract decorative background. Pretendard, Do Hyeon and Gowun Batang are distributed with their OFL licenses. Do Hyeon and Gowun Batang are converted from the official TTF files to WOFF2 without changing glyphs or font names.

The prototype does not call an AI or sales API, change existing product content, or publish anything. Uploads stay in browser memory; reloading discards them. JSON export contains text, template choice and photo filenames, not image data. Extraction and production persistence are separate follow-up work. See `docs/design/sales-templates/spec.md` for acceptance criteria and `tasks.md` for validation results.

An explicit demo button or `?demo=1` loads the repository's existing fictional farm copy and sample photographs. The studio and exported JSON identify the material as a design example, not actual sales evidence. Replacing existing content and clearing it require confirmation. The three layouts use different section order, typography, photo spacing and information hierarchy. Reference decisions and sources are recorded in the prototype README.

The latest revision follows the visual sequence of a Coupang seller detail page: a product cover, origin/variety/weight summary, alternating source paragraphs and photographs, optional composition and farm-update chapters, and optional packing/storage/shipping notes. Farmer Story places its cover photo before the title. Paragraphs split only at blank lines; additional photos retain their supplied order and captions. Unpaired text or photos remain visible. No seller imagery, copy, ratings, or claims are reused.

Composition and farm updates are optional source-text fields with separate headings. Photos follow story paragraphs, composition, then updates; the update chapter shows its photo first. Composition repeats the supplied weight. Paragraphs longer than 120 characters use readable 17px body type without rewriting or shortening the text. The explicit demo includes six existing repository photographs and original composition/update copy; packing, storage and shipping remain empty. Research now distinguishes the previously viewed Coupang product from six other Korean retailers’ full or partial public detail images. Additional Coupang products could not be visually reviewed because access returned HTTP 403. See `coupang-research.md` for exact coverage and source links.

## Product information coverage
Optional taste/texture, size/variation, quality records/certification, and harvest/dispatch notes now follow the basic product summary. Lower sections distinguish pre-purchase cautions/contact procedures, packaging/shipping, and storage/serving instructions. An editor-only list links to empty optional fields. It reports input presence, not factual verification, completeness, or publication eligibility. Existing transaction/refund policies are unchanged. The fictional demo adds only source sentences from scr-03/scr-04; missing grades, measured quality, certifications, dates, handling instructions, and policies are never invented. `content-analysis.md` compares the text and tables inside six reference images, separates seller claims from facts, and records missing Farmclub source material.
