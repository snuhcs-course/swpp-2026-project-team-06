# Farmclub

> Working name. The final team/service name is still being decided.

## Project

**AI-based demand-first agricultural matching.**
Consumers commit to produce before it is grown or harvested, and producers plan their supply around that confirmed demand. The service connects the two sides and helps producers share the story of their farm without having to create content themselves.
